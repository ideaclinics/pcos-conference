    FAQ

    Good to know

    Is this really free?+

    Yes, completely free. Register so we can send your joining link and the recording. It's health education, not individual medical advice.

    Is this part of the doctors' conference?+

    Yes. It is the public live stream of the 11 AM–12 PM panel at PMOS CME 2026 in Hyderabad, and anyone can watch online.

    Do I need a medical background to attend?+

    Not at all. It's designed for women, families and anyone curious about PMOS — explained in plain language.

    What if I can't attend live?+

    Register anyway — we'll email the recording to everyone who signs up.

    Is PMOS the same as PCOS?+

    Yes — PMOS is the terminology used in this programme, formerly and commonly known as PCOS.
