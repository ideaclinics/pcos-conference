ZioHeal® in diabetic foot care at home

ZioHeal® is a bioceramic wound patch based on a zeolite–iodine complex. Its manufacturer describes it as a sterile medical device intended to adsorb wound exudate while slow iodine release provides an aseptic environment, for acute and chronic surface wounds including diabetic foot ulcers.

Assess → Prepare → Apply ZioHeal® → Secondary dressing → Monitor and follow up.

Important: demonstrated under appropriate medical supervision. It does not replace vascular assessment, infection management, debridement, off-loading, glycaemic control or surgery when required.

  • Expert consultationsFoot surgeon and endocrinology support

  • Wound care and debridementBy trained professionals, when clinically indicated

Who may benefit
  • Numbness, burning or tingling in the feet
  • Recurrent foot wounds or a non-healing ulcer
  • Suspected or previous foot infection or ulcer
  • High-risk feet

FAQ

Who can attend in person?+

The workshop at Lemon Tree Premier, HITEC City is for doctors and healthcare professionals. Patients, caregivers and the public join through the live webcast.

How do I get the webcast link?+

It is sent to the mobile number or email you register with, before 15 November.

Does the webcast replace a doctor's visit?+

No. It is health education, not an individual medical consultation. If you have a foot wound, numbness or any foot concern, get your feet examined by a doctor.

When should I go to hospital immediately?+

Don't wait for the event if a foot wound shows spreading redness, swelling, pus, a foul smell, black or discoloured skin or severe pain, or if you have a wound and feel unwell or have a fever. This list is not complete: when in doubt, get it checked.

A small wound should never become a big problem.
