Why this matters

Diabetic foot problems often begin silently

Tap each step to see how a small, unnoticed problem can become an emergency.

Don't wait for a diabetic foot wound to become an emergency. Diabetes-related nerve damage and poor circulation make injuries hard to notice and slow to heal. Early detection breaks the chain.

For doctors · Live demonstration

The first time: ZioHeal® in diabetic foot care at home

Advanced wound-care technology, brought into Diabetes Care at Home.

ZioHeal® is a bioceramic wound patch based on a zeolite–iodine complex. Its manufacturer describes it as a sterile medical device in which the zeolite is intended to adsorb wound exudate while slow iodine release provides an aseptic environment, for acute and chronic surface wounds, including diabetic foot ulcers.

The emphasis is on integrating wound care with diabetes management, not treating the wound in isolation.

  1. Wound assessment
  2. Wound preparation
  3. ZioHeal® application
  4. Appropriate secondary dressing
  5. Monitoring and follow-up
Important: ZioHeal® is demonstrated as an advanced wound-care option under appropriate medical supervision. It is not a substitute for vascular assessment, infection management, debridement, off-loading, glycaemic control or surgical care when these are clinically required.

FAQ

Good to know

Who can attend in person?+

The workshop at Lemon Tree Premier, HITEC City is for doctors and healthcare professionals. Patients, caregivers and the public join through the live webcast.

How do I get the webcast link?+

After you register, the joining link is sent to the mobile number or email you provided, before 15 November.

Does the webcast replace a doctor's visit?+

No. It is health education and is not an individual medical consultation. If you have a foot wound, numbness or any foot concern, get your feet examined by a doctor.

When should I go to hospital immediately?+

Seek urgent care without waiting for the event if a foot wound shows spreading redness, swelling, pus, a foul smell, black or discoloured skin or severe pain, or if you have a wound and feel unwell or have a fever. This list is not complete: when in doubt, get it checked.

Is ZioHeal® a replacement for other wound care?+

No. It is demonstrated as one advanced wound-care option under medical supervision. It does not replace vascular assessment, infection management, debridement, off-loading, glycaemic control or surgery when needed.

What is Diabetes Foot Care at Home?+

An IDEA Clinics service combining specialist consultations, testing, home dressings and debridement, infection management, glucose monitoring and ongoing diabetes care for appropriate patients. See feet.ideaclinics.com for details.
