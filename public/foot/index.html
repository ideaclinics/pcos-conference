Why it matters

Diabetes complications can start without symptoms

  1. High sugar for years. Damage builds quietly.
  2. No early symptoms. Heart, kidneys and eyes often feel fine.
  3. Nerve damage. Numb feet hide small wounds.
  4. Found late. Treatment gets harder.
  5. Check early. Regular tests catch it in time.

Recognise early. Treat early. Prevent complications.

Can the public come to the venue?

The in-person programme is for doctors and healthcare professionals. Patients and families join online. Doctors can register at pmos.ideaclinics.com.

Does the webinar replace a doctor's visit?

No. If you have a foot wound, numbness, chest pain, vision changes or any worry about your health, see a doctor.

When is it an emergency?

Do not wait for the event. Go to a hospital for chest pain, breathlessness, sudden vision loss, or a foot wound with spreading redness, swelling, pus, a foul smell or black skin, or if you have a wound and a fever or feel unwell. When in doubt, get it checked.
