A foot wound can start without any pain

  1. Numbness. Nerve damage dulls feeling.
  2. A cut nobody notices. Small injuries go unseen.
  3. An ulcer. Poor circulation slows healing.
  4. Infection. Late treatment makes it worse.
  5. A limb at risk.

Recognise early. Treat early. Save the foot. Save the limb.

For doctors

A practical workshop on neuropathy screening, wound and infection care, off-loading and when to refer. It includes a live wound-care demonstration with ZioHeal®, and a panel on one question: can we prevent diabetic foot amputations?

ZioHeal® is a zeolite–iodine wound patch. It is shown as one option under medical supervision. It does not replace vascular assessment, infection control, debridement, off-loading, glucose control or surgery.

Care at home

For some patients, foot care can continue at home: specialist visits, dressings, debridement when needed, and glucose monitoring. It suits people with numb feet, a wound that is not healing, or difficulty travelling to a clinic.

Questions

Can the public come to the venue?

The workshop is for doctors and healthcare professionals. Patients and families join online.

Does the webcast replace a doctor's visit?

No. If you have a foot wound, numbness or any worry about your feet, have them examined by a doctor.

When is it an emergency?

Do not wait for the event. Go to a hospital if a foot wound has spreading redness, swelling, pus, a foul smell, black skin or severe pain, or if you have a wound and a fever or feel unwell. When in doubt, get it checked.
