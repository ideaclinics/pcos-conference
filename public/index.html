FAQ

Good to know

Can patients come to the venue?+

The in-person programme is for doctors and healthcare professionals. Patients and families join the diabetes complications webinar online.

Can I attend both halls?+

One registration covers the day. Choose the sessions that fit you from either hall.

Does the webinar replace a doctor's visit?+

No. Anyone with a foot wound, numbness, vision changes or any worry about their health should be examined by a doctor.
