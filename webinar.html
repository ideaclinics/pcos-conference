Myth or fact?

A quick preview of what gets cleared up in the session. Tap any card.

Claim

"PMOS just means you'll struggle to lose weight — that's it."

Tap to reveal →
Myth

PMOS affects metabolism, fertility, skin and mental health too — weight is only one part of the picture.

Claim

"If my periods are regular, I can't have PMOS."

Tap to reveal →
Myth

Some women with PMOS have regular cycles. Diagnosis looks at more than just periods.

Claim

"Every woman with PMOS needs an insulin test."

Tap to reveal →
Myth

Routine insulin tests aren't always useful — glucose testing is usually the more meaningful one. We'll explain why.

Claim

"PMOS has nothing to do with long-term health risk."

Tap to reveal →
Fact

PMOS is linked to higher long-term risk of diabetes and heart disease — which is exactly why early, informed care matters.
